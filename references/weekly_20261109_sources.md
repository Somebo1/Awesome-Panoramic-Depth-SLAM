## Verified method additions

- **PVDepth**, [formal ICML2026/PMLR306:114962–114986](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/song26u.html), [November5 arXiv deposit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07873v1), [official source](https://github.com/ChuanxinSong/PVDepth): complete ERP video input; internal co-centered cubemap temporal branch is not a raw-fisheye/pinhole interface or stereo baseline. Serialized output is affine-ambiguous inverse radial distance, not metric range or optical-axis Z. Inference, two-stage training and evaluation source and an ungated UNet model listing were verified. The model listing dates to July23; this is not a newly released November model. Separate pretrained base components and noncommercial/research license conditions remain. Bounded and legacy evaluation conversions differ after the same scale-and-shift alignment and must not be mixed. ViPE/MapAnything evaluation references are estimates, not independent depth/pose truth. See [full method/figure evidence](pvdepth_20261109_sources.md).
- **OpenVIO**, [October30 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41671v1), [actual MIT implementation](https://github.com/93won/openvio): overlap backfill with released pinhole, fisheye and stitched ERP entry points. ERP is monocular plus IMU; physical stereo is a separate pinhole/fisheye mode. Calibrated IMU and gravity provide metric local scale, not absolute global position/yaw. It produces sparse landmarks and a visual-inertial trajectory, not dense depth; local landmark reacquisition is not global loop closure. No local build/inference or input-rate-as-throughput claim. See [full source and figure audit](openvio_20261109_sources.md).

Both original PDFs are local/ignored. True original Figure3/PDFpage4 for PVDepth and Figure2/PDFpage2 for OpenVIO, their complete pages and final WebPs were individually visually inspected.
