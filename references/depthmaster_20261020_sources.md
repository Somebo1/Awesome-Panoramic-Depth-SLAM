# DepthMaster (2026) targeted source review

Checked: 2026-10-20. ID: `depthmaster2026`. This is the PolyU perspective/panorama model, distinct from the unrelated 2025 diffusion paper with the same short name.

- [Original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12368), [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.12368): 25-page downloaded PDF. Page 4 Figure 3 is the method overview; the original page and final crop were visually inspected. Page 7 Table 1, metric section: Stanford2D3D 7.73 vs DAP 10.89; Matterport3D 9.49 vs 17.78; PanoSUNCG 4.54 vs 6.71; mean 7.25 vs 11.79. These are the table display values. Pages 6 and 16 describe zero-shot full-ERP evaluation under DA2, six 518-square cubemap faces, 512x1024 reprojection and metric evaluation without GT scale/shift alignment. All three quantitative panorama datasets are indoor. No independent model run was performed.
- [Official project](https://polyu-vclab.github.io/DepthMaster-page/): unified pinhole/ERP scope and indoor/outdoor qualitative galleries. Its outdoor comparisons apply the authors' sky mask and extra range filtering to competing point clouds; the qualitative gallery is not evidence of a numerical outdoor win.
- [Official repository](https://github.com/PolyU-VCLab/DepthMaster): September 2026 NeurIPS acceptance announcement; no independent proceedings record verified. MIT code; released checkpoint; Linux/Python 3.10/PyTorch 2.4/CUDA 12.1 tested setup with pinned utils3d.
- [Perspective infer source](https://github.com/PolyU-VCLab/DepthMaster/blob/main/depthmaster/model/model.py): depth is point Z, scaled by metric_scale; optional fov_x, normalized centered intrinsics, normals and validity mask. infer_panorama() convenience wrapper currently returns forward outputs without applying metric_scale. Do not treat that raw wrapper point map as metric solely from its name.
- [ERP evaluator](https://github.com/PolyU-VCLab/DepthMaster/blob/main/eval_panorama/eval.py): accepts complete ERP externally, forms six overlapping 95-degree virtual faces, multiplies raw points by metric_scale, obtains range by Euclidean point norm, then blends back to ERP. Virtual rotations are known projection conditions, not estimated trajectories. Full-ERP input is tagged direct at the external interface; internal conversion is explained in its support note. No raw fisheye interface was verified.
- [Evaluation config](https://github.com/PolyU-VCLab/DepthMaster/blob/main/eval_panorama/configs/eval_panorama.json) defaults to dm_scale/dm_affine. [Evaluation helper](https://github.com/PolyU-VCLab/DepthMaster/blob/main/eval_panorama/eval/utils/infer.py) requires evaluation.metric_depth_eval=true to compute unaligned metric results before alignment. The lower-clamp numerical guard and validity masks remain part of source implementation.
- [Official model metadata](https://huggingface.co/VCLab-PolyU/DepthMaster): MIT, ungated, depthmaster.pt exists. Metadata hash add341b64ae9f90372b6483b334ac496a18fe081. No weights downloaded.
