## PanoRecon: paper-described companion benchmark, pending full data access

- [Original HoloRecon v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36876v1), [Section4 and AppendixA.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.36876v1), [official project](https://zhuoxiaoli.github.io/HoloRecon_page/).
- Seven real outdoor routes with repeated architecture/revisits. Mean duration11.49min; mean length0.87km (0.26–1.69km). X4 video is 5K/30fps, exported ERP, sampled2K at0.1s. These are paper descriptions, not a checked downloadable image inventory.
- Panorama and RTK-assisted LiDAR sessions are captured independently along planned routes. RTK-corrected LiDAR SLAM and onboard camera calibration define fixed reference cameras; RealityScan matches perspective ERP rigs and estimates ERP poses. References are estimated/registered, not independent tracked camera truth. 0.1917px is a reprojection residual, not a position error bound.
- The project has previews, videos and a method reconstruction viewer, but no established full benchmark archive, downloadable per-image depth definition/masks, official split or dataset license. Do not turn those displays into a full-release claim. Depth and trajectory availability remain unknown; trajectory provenance is estimated. Curation pending at bottom.
- Dataset preview is the official seven-scene LiDAR-reference montage `assets/web/dataset_full.webp`, not a method-estimated map. Original2400×949 SHA-256 `760172330a716182244ea83af5c233ed2bb71580842440a23bf48f309a019327`; published1600×633 plus720×285 thumb. Original and final visually inspected.
