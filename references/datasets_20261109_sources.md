- The public card documents126696 frames,117 clips,67 routes,8 outdoor towns. Town01/03/04/05/06/07 are training(112988 frames/95clips/54routes); Town02/10 are held out(13708frames/22clips/13routes). PNG/NPY raw data are provided only for Town02/10 at1024x512, not all training towns referenced by setting_dynamic.json. Frame totals are author-card counts, not locally opened HDF5 counts.
- [Actual shared conversion source](https://github.com/ChuanxinSong/PanoCARLA/blob/main/stage2/equi_extract_for_fov100_multiprocess.py) converts optical-axis Z to Euclidean ray distance before ERP face selection. Thus depth is rendered radial range, not ERP Z. A1000m source depth scale/far plane is not a valid evaluation cutoff. Task-specific sky, far-plane and positive/finite masks remain explicit.
- The released pose representation is(x,y,z,yaw): CARLA-world metres and yaw in degrees, pitch/roll fixed to zero. Paper/project6DoF wording must not turn this into arbitrary orientation diversity or real independent tracking truth. Six co-centered virtual views yield ERP, not known-baseline stereo. Annotate depth=yes/rendered, trajectory=yes/rendered with those constraints.
- [Static card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Soon122/PanoCARLA-Static) confirms shared routes but regenerated altitude motion; variants are not pixel/pose matched. Static remains an independent existing entry. Dynamic access is gated; Static is ungated. The dataset terms are CC BY4.0, distinct from collection-code MIT/third-party licensing. No gate was bypassed.
- Reading priority is specialized, after established/common benchmarks; this is a targeted synthetic outdoor video depth/SLAM resource, not a mature real-sensor accuracy benchmark.
